# Privacy Policy - Muster Roll Automation for VB-G RAM G (Unofficial)

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

* It runs only on pages of https://vbgramgde2.dord.gov.in/ that you open.
* Excel/CSV files you upload are read locally in your browser.
* Settings, the batch queue, progress and logs are kept in your browser's local extension storage and can be wiped with "Reset storage & logs" or by removing the extension.
* PDFs and Excel reports are saved only to your own Downloads folder.
* No analytics, no remote servers, no third-party code, no remote scripts.
* The extension is unofficial and is not affiliated with any government body.

Contact: hoquevarieties@gmail.com.
